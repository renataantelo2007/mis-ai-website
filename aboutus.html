What I Learned From This Assignment

In this assignment, I learned that Management Information Systems (MIS) play an important role in processing, organizing, and managing information to support decision making. I also gained a good understanding of how businesses rely on information systems to improve daily operations, efficiency, and productivity. When exploring AI, I learned that AI can be a valuable tool for students and professionals when used ethically and without replacing independent human thinking. I realized that AI ethics is extremely important because irresponsible use can lead to misinformation, academic dishonesty, and a lack of accountability. Additionally, I learned about different cybersecurity threats, such as malware and ransomware, and the importance of protecting sensitive information. Lastly, creating this website helped me develop basic HTML coding skills, including organizing webpages, adding images, creating hyperlinks, and designing a functional website from scratch.
