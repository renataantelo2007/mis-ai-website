Correct AI Usage

AI can be a helpful tool whenever students us it to help their understanding of already learned academic material. Even though it can help better understanding of difficult topics and provide feedback, students should still develop their own complete independent work. AI generated information should be verified it can have mistakes and confuse students rather than benefiting them. Students should also follow the professor's AI rules and make it clear when AI help is used.

Incorrect AI Usage

Examples of Incorrect AI Usage

Plagiarism
Submitting AI-generated assignments as your own work without acknowledging AI assistance.

Unauthorized AI Assistance
Using AI on assignments when the instructor has specifically prohibited its use.

Spreading Misinformation
Sharing AI-generated information without verifying whether it is accurate.

My Opinion on AI Misuse

Although AI can be useful, improper use can create ethical problems in various areas of society. When AI is used incorrectly in the workplace, it can negatively affect businesses through inaccurate information and dishonest practices, especially when AI assistance is prohibited. In educational environments, excessive reliance on AI can prevent students from fully understanding and applying academic material, causing them to fall behind. Lastly, in everyday life, relying too heavily on AI instead of building real-world relationships can weaken human connections.
