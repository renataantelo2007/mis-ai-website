Malware and Cyber Threats

The WannaCry Ransomware Attack of 2017

On May 12, 2017, the WannaCry ransomware attack began spreading across computers worldwide. The malware encrypted victims' files and demanded payment to restore access. The attack disrupted many organizations, including hospitals and businesses. This event was significant because it demonstrated the importance of cybersecurity, software updates, and protecting sensitive data.
